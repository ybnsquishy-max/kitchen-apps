Brain

On your home screen, JARVIS thinks with your own Claude API key from console.anthropic.com. Each message costs about 1 US cent on your Anthropic account, a little more with photos. The key is kept only on this phone and is only ever sent to Anthropic.

Protocols

A protocol is a phrase JARVIS acts on instantly. Add sites to open, or switch his voice mode.
