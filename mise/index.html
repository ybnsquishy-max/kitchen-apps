Share Mise

Everyone you share with sees the shared list. They also get My copy, their own version that updates when you add or remove things and keeps their own changes.

  1. Tap Share at the top of this page in Claude.
  2. Invite people by email. Editor lets them change the shared list. Viewer lets them see it and keep their own copy.
  3. Turn off "Anyone with the link". Changes can't be saved while it's on.

You can also send someone a file copy of everything, or bring in a backup from the Pantry app.
