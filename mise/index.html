Handwriting & whiteboards (optional Claude key)

Printed recipe books are read on this phone for free. Handwriting and whiteboards need Claude. To use it, paste your own API key from console.anthropic.com. Each photo costs about 2–5 cents on your Anthropic account. With a key, Claude also watches videos (about 5–10 cents each), which reads them better and works for videos with no talking. The key is kept only on this phone, and is only ever sent to Anthropic.
