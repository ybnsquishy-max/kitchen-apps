About Dash

Dash is a car mode for your phone. Connect your phone to the car with Bluetooth or a cable, so music and calls play through the car's speakers. Dash gives you big buttons for maps, music, calls and messages.

It can't show on the car's own screen (that needs Apple CarPlay or Android Auto), answer incoming calls, or control Spotify or Apple Music. Those open in their own apps.

Maps © OpenStreetMap contributors. Place search by Photon, routes by OSRM, weather by Open-Meteo. Your places, favourites and songs stay on this phone.

Dash

Car mode for your phone.

    Set Dash up while you're parked, and mount your phone where you can see it without holding it.

    For full screen, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    Dash asks for your location to show the map, your speed and the weather. Dash has no account, and your places, favourites and songs stay on this phone.

    Typing is paused while you drive

    Use Home, Work or a recent place, or tap the microphone and say where you're going. If someone else is typing, tap below.
