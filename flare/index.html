What Flare can and can't do

It can dial the emergency number, open a message to your people with a map link of where you are, make a noise, fake a call, and hold your medical details for whoever helps you.

It can't send a message by itself — your phone always shows it to you first, and you tap send. It can't listen or track you in the background, and it won't work with no signal or no battery. It is not a replacement for calling 000.

Everything you put in Flare stays on this phone.
